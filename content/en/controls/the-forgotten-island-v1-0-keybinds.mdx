## Running in The Forgotten Island v1.0: The Left Shift Rule

Running is the single most common control complaint for this build. According to community reports, holding **Left Shift** makes your character sprint, while **Right Shift** does nothing at all. That asymmetry is unusual enough that players assumed their keyboard was broken.

The developer acknowledged the issue directly and said it would be corrected in a later version. Until you are on a build where both Shift keys work, train yourself to reach for the left one.

| Situation | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hold Left Shift | Character runs | Use this as your default |
| Hold Right Shift | No sprint | Switch to Left Shift |
| Hold Shift, nothing happens | Keyboard input method may not be English | Switch your input method to English and retry |
| Sprint drains too fast | Stops and starts feel jerky | Combine short sprints with walking to keep the camera stable |

That third row is worth repeating because it is easy to miss. The developer noted that some players could not run because their system input method had been switched to a non-English layout, which intercepted the keystroke before the game saw it. If Shift seems dead, check your language bar before you file a bug report.

## Mouse Sensitivity and Camera Control

The second big complaint in early player experience was camera instability. Players reported that the default mouse sensitivity was far too high to hold a steady line while running or walking, making the view swing unpredictably.

The developer responded that a mouse sensitivity option would be added in a later version. If you are playing a build that still lacks the slider, you can compensate at the operating-system or mouse-driver level.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Camera whips around when you nudge the mouse | Sensitivity set too high | Lower the in-game slider, or lower your mouse DPI |
| Aim drifts after you stop moving | Mouse acceleration enabled | Turn off "Enhance pointer precision" in Windows mouse settings |
| Fine adjustments feel impossible | Very high DPI with no in-game scaling | Drop DPI in your mouse software, then re-test in game |
| Sensitivity feels different every session | Different profiles loading per game | Save one dedicated profile for this title |

### A Quick Sensitivity Tuning Routine

1. Stand somewhere safe, away from enemies, with a clear horizon.
2. Sweep the camera left and right at a normal walking pace.
3. Adjust sensitivity until a full sweep of the mouse pad turns you roughly 180 degrees.
4. Sprint in a straight line and confirm the horizon stays level.
5. Save the profile and avoid changing it mid-run.

This routine takes about three minutes and solves most of the "unstable camera" complaints that show up in community reports.

## Using Food and Potions: The Inventory Controls Nobody Explains

Consumables confuse a lot of new players because the game does not use a hotbar. According to the developer, the flow is straightforward once you know it: purchase the item into your backpack first, then double-click it in the backpack to consume it.

| Step | Action | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buy the potion or food so it transfers to your backpack | Trying to use it from the shop screen |
| 2 | Open your backpack | Assuming a hotbar slot exists |
| 3 | Double-click the item | Single-clicking and expecting it to trigger |
| 4 | Confirm the effect applied | Clicking repeatedly and wasting a second item |

The double-click requirement is the part that catches people out. If a potion appears to do nothing, check whether you actually bought it into your inventory before you try again.

## FAQ

**Can I change The Forgotten Island v1.0 keybinds in the options menu?**
Mouse sensitivity is the setting that was added following player feedback. Other bindings are not publicly documented as rebindable, so check your own build's options menu first, and use a keyboard-level remap if nothing is available in game.

**Why does Right Shift not make my character run?**
Community reports say only Left Shift triggers sprinting in this build. The developer acknowledged the limitation and said it would be addressed in a later version, so use Left Shift for now.

**Why does Shift do nothing at all?**
The developer noted that a non-English keyboard input method can block the keystroke. Switch your system input method to English and try again before assuming the binding is broken.

**How do I eat food or drink a potion?**
Buy the item so it lands in your backpack, open the backpack, then double-click the item to consume it. Single-clicking will not work.
