## What Kind of Game Is The Forgotten Island v1.0?

The premise is deliberately simple. You open an ancient scroll, wake up on a forgotten island, and find medieval buildings, merchants, and villagers around you. Your first goals are basic survival — drink water, eat food — and then exploration, with the long-term objective of finding a way back to the real world.

The core loop mixes several systems that usually live in separate genres:

- **Exploration:** Roam a small open world and interact with NPCs, wells, beds, and food sources.
- **Combat:** Challenge NPCs to duels, level up, and earn talent points.
- **Tasks:** Each level can be completed by finishing the assigned objectives.
- **Life skills:** Farming, fishing, eating, and drinking are all available, and the game explicitly leaves it to you to decide how you enjoy it.

Controls are minimal, which makes the game easy to pick up but easy to misread if you skip the tutorial text.

| Action | Input | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recharge vitality | Hold E beside food or a bed | Restores vitality |
| Recharge stamina | Hold E beside a well | Restores stamina |
| Start a fight | Hold F beside an NPC | Begins a duel |
| Get stronger | Level up and buy equipment | Raises your combat stats |

Combat follows a readable set of rules, and knowing them early saves a lot of frustration.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Light or heavy attack | Reduces enemy vitality but consumes your stamina |
| Defend | Blocks enemy attacks without spending stamina |
| Relax | Restores stamina, but leaves you with no defense |
| Recovery while hit | If an enemy strikes you, your recovery does nothing |

Talent points are the real progression system, and each stat pushes you toward a different playstyle.

| Point spent | Bonus gained |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Strength | +120 Vitality, +50 Stamina |
| 1 Agility | +5 Attack, +2 Armor |
| 1 Intelligence | +20 Crit, +20 Avoid, +40 Lucky |

### Practical tips for your first hour

- **Bank stamina before every duel.** Attacking drains it, and running dry mid-fight leaves you stuck defending.
- **Defend instead of relaxing when an enemy is mid-swing.** Relaxing restores stamina but removes your defense entirely.
- **Lean into Strength early if you keep dying.** Vitality and stamina keep you alive long enough to learn attack timing.
- **Use wells liberally.** They are the fastest way to top up stamina between fights.
- **Pick one damage stat and commit.** Splitting points between Agility and Intelligence slows your power spike.
