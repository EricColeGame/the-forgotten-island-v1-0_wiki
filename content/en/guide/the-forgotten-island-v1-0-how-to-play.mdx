## The Forgotten Island v1.0 How to Play: The Core Loop

If you internalize nothing else about The Forgotten Island v1.0 how to play, internalize the loop. Every productive session follows the same four-stage rhythm, and players who skip the last stage are the ones who end up stuck, lost, or frustrated.

| Stage | What you should be doing | How you know it is done |
|---|---|---|
| **Orient** | Walk the immediate area, note landmarks, test your controls | You can name three landmarks without checking a map |
| **Scout** | Push outward in one direction until something new appears | You have found at least one point of interest away from your start |
| **Objective** | Commit to the task the game has surfaced for you | The objective updates or a new area opens |
| **Consolidate** | Save, restock, and review what you learned | You know exactly where you are going next session |

The fourth stage is the one people forget. It costs you two minutes and saves you twenty. Before you close the game, write down — physically or in a note on your phone — the one thing you were about to do next. When you come back, you will not spend the first half hour re-learning your own intentions.

### Why orientation beats speed

The Forgotten Island v1.0 rewards players who look up, look behind, and check side paths. Speeding toward the next objective usually means walking past the very item or shortcut you will need later. Treat the first fifteen minutes of any new area as a scouting run rather than a progress run.
