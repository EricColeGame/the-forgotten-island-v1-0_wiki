## Survival Secrets: Water, Food, and Stamina

The Steam store description is upfront that hydration and nourishment come before exploration. That is not flavor text; it is the pacing mechanism. Vitals quietly gate how much you can accomplish in a session.

| Vital | How You Restore It | Practical Priority |
|---|---|---|

| Stamina | Resting, relaxed states, pacing your actions | Combat-critical |

## Combat Secrets: Light, Heavy, Defend, Relax

Combat here is turn-based and stamina-driven. The mechanic that separates comfortable players from struggling ones is understanding that every action has an opportunity cost.

| Action | Stamina Cost | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| Light strike | Low | Chipping health, preserving your pool |
| Heavy strike | High | Finishing a weakened enemy |
| Defend | Low / none | Absorbing a known big hit |
| Relax | Recovers stamina | Resetting after a heavy exchange |

The rhythm that works: open with light strikes, drop into a relaxed or defensive state when your stamina dips, then commit to heavy strikes once the enemy is low. Burning heavy attacks early leaves you flat-footed against the counterattack, which is where most early deaths happen.

## Talent Secrets: Strength, Agility, and Intelligence

Character growth runs through a multi-tier talent layout weighted across Strength, Agility, and Intelligence. The temptation is to spread points evenly "just in case." Resist it. The island rewards a defined identity far more than a balanced one.

| Attribute | What It Generally Supports | Pick It If You Want |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Heavier physical output | Short, decisive fights |
| Agility | Speed and evasion-leaning play | Fewer incoming hits |
| Intelligence | Utility and scaling options | Flexibility across map tiers |

A practical approach: choose one primary attribute and one secondary, then commit for the first several levels. You can broaden later once your core loop is stable. Players who split three ways early tend to feel weak in every encounter, which makes the survival layer feel harsher than it actually is.

The real trick is overlap. Farm and fish on the way to a quest objective instead of making dedicated trips, then sell everything in one visit to the merchant area. That single habit cuts a surprising amount of backtracking out of a run.
